# ELEVA — Connecting Education With Opportunity

Eleva is a digital platform designed to connect **educational institutions, students, and recruiters** through one centralized career ecosystem.

> **From Campus to Career, Connected.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Eleva aims to bridge the gap between educational institutions and the industry by creating a structured platform for student opportunities and recruitment.

The platform connects three major stakeholders:

* 🏫 Educational Institutions
* 👨‍🎓 Students
* 🏢 Recruiters / Companies

Eleva is designed to support multiple institutions and multiple recruiters while providing students with better access to career opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The current placement process can involve disconnected communication and manual coordination between colleges, students, and companies.

Some common challenges include:

* Difficulty connecting students with relevant opportunities
* Manual placement coordination
* Limited interaction between institutions and recruiters
* Difficulty managing student career information
* Challenges for recruiters in finding suitable candidates
* Lack of a centralized recruitment ecosystem

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Eleva provides a centralized platform that connects institutions with recruiters and helps students move from education toward employment.

The platform creates a structured ecosystem where:

**Institutions → Students → Recruiters → Opportunities → Hiring**

### Institutions

Institutions are onboarded through Eleva and can manage their students and placement activities.

### Students

Students are added to Eleva by their respective institutions. They can sign in and access their profiles and available opportunities.

### Recruiters

Recruiters are onboarded through Eleva and can discover suitable candidates and manage hiring requirements.

---

## 👥 User Roles

### 🏫 Institutions

Institutions can use Eleva to:

* Manage student profiles
* Coordinate placement activities
* Connect with recruiters
* Manage opportunities
* Track applications
* Monitor placement outcomes

Institutions are onboarded through Eleva rather than using public registration.

---

### 👨‍🎓 Students

Students are added to Eleva by their institutions.

Students can:

* Sign in to their accounts
* Maintain career profiles
* Add skills and projects
* View relevant opportunities
* Apply for opportunities
* Track application status
* Access future learning resources

Students do not independently register on the platform.

---

### 🏢 Recruiters

Recruiters are onboarded through Eleva.

Recruiters can:

* Create hiring requirements
* Discover suitable candidates
* Review student profiles
* Shortlist candidates
* Coordinate interviews
* Track recruitment progress

Recruiters do not freely self-register.

---

## 🔐 Controlled Onboarding

Eleva follows a controlled onboarding model instead of unrestricted public registration.

### Institution

```text
Eleva Approaches Institution
        ↓
Institution Onboarding
        ↓
Institution Account
        ↓
Student Management
```

### Student

```text
Institution Adds Student
        ↓
Student Account Created
        ↓
Student Signs In
```

### Recruiter

```text
Eleva Approaches Company
        ↓
Recruiter Onboarding
        ↓
Recruiter Account
        ↓
Talent Discovery
```

This model helps create a more structured and trusted ecosystem.
